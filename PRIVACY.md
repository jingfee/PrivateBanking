# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-05_

PrivateBanking is a private, non-commercial application used solely by its owner to view their own bank and card transactions.

## What data is accessed
- Account information and transaction history from the owner's own accounts, retrieved via Enable Banking (PSD2 account information service).
- Read-only access. The application does not initiate payments.

## How data is stored
- Data is stored only on the owner's self-hosted server (home lab), accessible solely via the owner's private network.
- No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising.

## Who has access
- Only the owner (and possibly one member of the owner's household).

## Retention and deletion
- Data is retained for as long as the owner chooses and can be deleted at any time. Bank consent can be revoked at any time through the bank or Enable Banking.
